# Parcel Plane

A short physics delivery game about folding a paper plane and throwing it onto the right porch.

## Pitch

You are a rooftop courier who never leaves the roof. Fold a paper plane with three sliders — nose weight, wing tilt, and throw power — then launch it across the alley. Thread clotheslines, dodge pigeons, and land on the marked doormat before the wind carries the mail into the street. Each successful delivery unlocks a slightly taller roof and a slightly worse gust.

## Features

- **Fold, then throw.** Three sliders change how the plane flies: nose weight (dive vs. float), wing tilt (left/right drift), and throw power (launch speed).
- **Living alley.** Moving clotheslines, open windows, and pigeons that flap into your path mid-flight.
- **Porch targets.** Score rings on each landing: doormat (best), stoop, railing, or “in the bushes.”
- **Readable wind.** A wind sock on every roof shows the current gust so you can adjust the fold before you throw.
- **Ten roofs.** A short campaign of handmade launches, plus two plane upgrades (stiffer wings, extra fold slot) earned by clean landings.

## Prototype scope

First playable is one roof, one plane, sliders, a wind sock, and three landing zones. No character controller, no inventory, no dialogue — just aim, release, and watch the paper fly.

## Controls (prototype)

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Sliders / number keys | Set nose weight, wing tilt, throw power |
| Click-drag back, release | Launch |
| R | Reset throw |

Built as a Unity physics prototype in the same family as a demolition / projectile toy: one body in the air, a few moving obstacles, and a clear landing score.
